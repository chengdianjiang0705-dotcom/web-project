A LITTLE KNOW-HOW, A LOT OF POSSIBILITY

Get to know your colors.

Three simple ideas to take the guesswork out of getting dressed.
Think of them as starting points. Your taste gets the final say.

从中性色、同色系和一个亮点色开始，找到自己的感觉。

i

Color is personal. These are ideas to try, not rules to follow. Materials, lighting and your own preferences all shape the result.

01 / THE EASY START

Find your common ground.

中性色组合 · 简单，也有层次

Off-white, gray, charcoal and beige are an easy place to begin. Try a warm beige top with dark trousers, then bring a little light back in with off-white shoes.

You can explore contrast without adding a strong hue: light against dark gives familiar pieces a clear shape.

02 / SAME FAMILY, NEW DEPTH

Stay in your element.

同色系组合 · 用深浅变化制造层次

Choose different shades from one color family. A light blue top and navy trousers feel related, while the change in depth keeps the pieces distinct.

A gray shoe gives the palette a quiet pause. Try a lighter or darker version in the lab and see which balance you enjoy.

Try this combination ↗
03 / ONE PIECE TAKES THE LEAD

A little color goes a long way.

一个突出颜色 · 给喜欢的颜色一点空间

Pick one piece to be the focal point, and keep the other two simple. A terracotta top adds warmth to charcoal trousers and off-white shoes.

It doesn't have to be your top. Try putting the brighter color on your shoes, or swap the accent for your own favorite shade.
